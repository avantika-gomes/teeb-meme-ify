# Privacy Policy for Teeb Meme-ify

**Effective date:** August 9, 2026

Teeb Meme-ify ("the app") turns your photos into memes by generating caption
suggestions. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising. It does not use
analytics, crash reporting, or any third-party tracking software.

## What the app uses

**Photos.** Photos you take in the app or choose from your photo library.

**Text you type.** Any wording you enter to steer the caption suggestions.

**Date, time, and city of the photo.** If a photo you select has location and
timestamp information saved in it by your camera, the app reads that
information and uses it as background context for the captions. The
coordinates are converted into a city name using Apple's geocoding service.
Only the city name is used; the exact coordinates are never sent to the caption
service.

## What leaves your device

To generate captions, the app sends the following to OpenAI over an encrypted
connection:

- the photo you selected, resized to a smaller copy
- the text you typed, if any
- a short context line containing the day, time, and city described above

OpenAI processes this to produce caption suggestions and returns them to the
app. OpenAI's handling of this data is governed by its own privacy policy, at
<https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy>.

Nothing else is transmitted anywhere. There is no server operated by the
developer of this app, and no copy of your photos is kept off your device.

## What stays on your device

Photos you import and memes you create are saved in the app's own storage on
your device, along with the capture date and city for each photo. This
information is not backed up to any service by the app. Deleting the app
removes all of it.

When you share or save a meme, it goes wherever you send it using the standard
iOS share sheet.

## What the app does not do

- It does not sell or share your information with anyone for advertising.
- It does not track you across other apps or websites.
- It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, or device
  identifiers.
- It does not create a profile of you.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Your choices

You control which photos the app can reach through the iOS Photos permission,
which you can change at any time in Settings. To remove everything the app has
stored, delete the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new
effective date.
